Our Story

Netstock Solutions was founded by Mr. Shammun, a seasoned professional with a decade-long legacy of excellence in retail stock auditing. With a distinguished academic background in law and commerce (BA.LLB), Mr. Shammun brings a unique blend of expertise and insight to the industry.

Having spent over 10 years in leadership roles with prominent brands and franchises, Mr. Shammun identified a need for specialized retail stock audit services that combine expertise, innovation, and reliability. This vision led to the establishment of Netstock Solutions, which has since conducted over 2500 successful store audits across diverse retail segments.
